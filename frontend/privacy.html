Privacy policy

Your data, explained plainly.

This notice explains what BharatSec collects, why, and the rights you have under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Last updated 26 October 2026

Who we are

BharatSec (bharatsec.vercel.app) is operated by its founder, Devesh Jain, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. For any privacy question or request, contact our grievance officer, Devesh Jain, at deveshjain718@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you create an account

  • Username, display name and, if you add one, your email address.
  • Your password, stored only as a salted hash. We cannot read it.
  • If you sign in with Google, GitHub, Microsoft or Facebook: your account ID, name and email from that provider.

When you use BharatSec

  • The domains you scan and the results, the assets you monitor, and the alerts and incidents you create.
  • Messages you send to BharatSec AI. They are sent to our AI provider to produce an answer; we do not keep your conversations on our servers after the answer is delivered.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics.
  • Basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) kept by our hosting provider for security and abuse prevention. Rate limiting stores a one-way hash of your IP address, not the address itself.

Your browser also stores your theme choice and a few display preferences locally. These never leave your device.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you asked for: scans, monitoring, alerts, AI answers.
  • To keep accounts secure and prevent abuse.
  • To answer your messages and requests.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Who processes it for us

  • Vercel: hosting the website and application.
  • Neon: the database holding accounts, scans and monitoring data.
  • Google (Gemini API): generating AI answers to the messages you send.
  • MongoDB Atlas (Mumbai): BharatSec AI's reference knowledge base. It holds public reference material, not your personal data.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: delivering fonts and a script library to your browser.
  • The sign-in provider you choose, if you use social sign-in.

Some of these providers process data outside India. We choose providers with strong security practices and share only what each service needs.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account is active.
  • Scan, monitoring and incident data: until you delete it or your account is deleted.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep something longer.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it, and withdraw any consent you have given;
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights if you cannot;
  • resolve a grievance. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Email deveshjain718@gmail.com with the subject Privacy request. We aim to respond within 7 days.

Security

We protect data with HTTPS, hashed passwords, signed sessions, a strict content security policy and access controls. See our security page for details.

Children

BharatSec is meant for businesses and professionals, and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date below and, for significant changes, tell signed-in users.
