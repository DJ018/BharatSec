How to read this estimate

Expected annual loss is the sum of scenario likelihood × modelled one-time impact. Relevant findings adjust baseline likelihood using directional severity lifts (Critical 8%, High 4%, Medium 2%, Low 0.8% per finding). Inputs are organisation-supplied planning assumptions; results are not a prediction, insurance quote, legal opinion or compliance assessment. This is a decision-support view: validate assumptions with finance, legal and risk owners before making decisions.
