Free SSL certificate checker

See whether a website's certificate is valid, when it expires and how strong its TLS setup is.

Free and passive: we only read public information. Check only domains you own or are authorised to assess.

What this checks

  • Validity: whether browsers will trust the certificate.
  • Expiry: how many days are left. An expired certificate puts a full-page warning in front of every visitor.
  • TLS grade: whether old, weak protocol versions are still accepted.

How to fix common problems

  • Expiring soon: renew now, and turn on automatic renewal (free with Let's Encrypt and most hosts).
  • Low grade: allow only TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3 in your web server, load balancer or CDN settings.
  • Not valid: make sure the certificate covers the exact domain name and includes the full chain.

Frequently asked questions

Is this check safe to run?

Yes. It opens one normal HTTPS connection, the same as a browser visiting the site.

How often should I check my certificate?

Monthly, or set up BharatSec monitoring so you are warned before it expires.

Why does TLS 1.0 or 1.1 matter?

They are deprecated and have known weaknesses. Modern browsers and PCI DSS expect TLS 1.2 or newer.
