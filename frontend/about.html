About

Security that Indian businesses can actually use.

BharatSec shows a company what the internet can see about it, explains the risk in plain language and in rupees, and tells it what to fix first.

Why BharatSec exists

India's small and mid-sized businesses are moving online faster than ever, and the law now asks more of them: CERT-In expects incidents to be reported within 6 hours, and the DPDP Act puts real duties and penalties on anyone holding personal data. Most of these companies have no security team. Enterprise tools are priced and written for people who already know what a DMARC record or an exposed subdomain is.

BharatSec is built for everyone else. Enter a domain and in about a minute you see your score, the few things that matter most, how an attacker could chain them together, and what doing nothing could cost.

What we build

  • Domain scanner: passive checks of DNS, WHOIS, email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), SSL/TLS, security headers and subdomains, with a 0 to 100 score and a prioritised fix list.
  • SOC: website and asset monitoring, alerts and incident tracking.
  • Threat Intel: actively exploited vulnerabilities from the CISA KEV catalog, mapped to MITRE ATT&CK.
  • Cloud: read-only security posture checks for AWS, Azure and Google Cloud.
  • Risk: findings turned into expected annual loss in rupees.
  • BharatSec AI: an assistant that answers security and Indian compliance questions with cited sources.

How we work

  • Passive by default. Scans read only public information. We never attack, exploit or overload anyone's systems.
  • Privacy first. No advertising, no tracking cookies, no selling of data. See our privacy policy.
  • India first. CERT-In, the DPDP Act and rupee impact are built in, not bolted on.
  • Honest results. A score is a starting point, not a guarantee, and we say so.
