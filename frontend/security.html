Security

How we protect BharatSec, and how to report a problem.

A security product has to hold itself to a higher standard. Here is what we do today, and how researchers can reach us.

How we protect the platform

  • Encryption in transit: HTTPS everywhere with HSTS.
  • Passwords: never stored in plain text; salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes only.
  • Sessions: signed, HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite cookies, with rate limiting on sign-in.
  • Browser hardening: a strict Content Security Policy, frame blocking, and Permissions and Referrer policies.
  • Safe scanning: scans are passive and read public information only. Guards block scans of private and internal network addresses.
  • Least privilege: cloud checks use read-only audit permissions.
  • Testing: an automated test suite runs against every change.

Report a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue in BharatSec, email deveshjain718@gmail.com with the subject Security report. Please include:

  • what you found and where (URL or endpoint);
  • steps to reproduce, and the impact you believe it has;
  • how we can reach you for follow-up.

Our promises

  • We will acknowledge your report within 3 working days and keep you updated.
  • We will not take legal action against good-faith research that follows these rules.
  • With your permission, we will credit you once the issue is fixed.

Please don't

  • run denial-of-service tests, spam, or automated scanning that degrades the service;
  • access, change or delete data that isn't yours, beyond the minimum needed to show the issue;
  • use social engineering or physical attacks;
  • share the issue publicly before we have had a reasonable chance to fix it.
