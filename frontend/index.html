Platform capabilities

Six pillars of digital security intelligence

From raw reconnaissance to AI-powered insight, BharatSec covers every dimension of your digital risk.

Attack surface discovery

Map subdomains, open ports, DNS records and internet-facing services: exactly what attackers see from outside.

Web security audit

Check SSL/TLS, HTTP security headers, HTTPS enforcement and web defences to close the most common entry points.

Identity & credential risk

Surface leaked credentials, email authentication gaps (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and WHOIS exposure that enable phishing.

Configuration assessment

Catch misconfigured servers, weak TLS ciphers and exposed cloud storage before attackers do, with clear fixes.

Attack path mapping

See how weaknesses chain into real attack scenarios, and break the chain at its weakest link.

AI-powered insights

Every finding in plain English, with business impact and step-by-step remediation. No specialist required.
